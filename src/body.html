How to read this. A closing rank is the rank of the last candidate allotted a seat of that category, branch and institute in that round. Reserved-category candidates can also take UR and open category (OCS) seats, so the checker counts those too. IP seats are only for graduates of that AIIMS, and INST seats only for JIPMER graduates. Ratings: strong means you would have got the seat in Round 1. Good means a seat went to someone at your rank or worse in some round. Borderline means you are within 15% past the last rank allotted. The result files name MCh, DM and MDS courses but not MD or MS. A branch is typed MD or MS only when it is taught under that one degree; Anatomy, Obstetrics & Gynaecology and Ophthalmology are awarded as MD at some institutes and MS at others, so they are listed as Undefined. Branch names differ between institutes (for example Medicine and General Medicine), so they are grouped under one name. Seats under special quotas (RA, NED, KD) are listed in All allotments but not used in the ratings. Past closing ranks guide you; they don't guarantee a seat.
