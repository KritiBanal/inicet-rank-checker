How the estimate is made
Closing rank
The last rank allotted a seat of that category at that college, in any round (Round 1, Round 2 or the open round). Categories follow the seat's roster point, not the candidate's own category.
Estimated final close
A weighted average of past final closing ranks: January 2026 counts 45%, July 2026 35% and January 2025 20%. Sessions where the seat wasn't allotted are left out and the weights rescaled.
Estimated Round 1 close
The same weighting applied to Round 1 closing ranks.
Confidence
Higher: all three sessions had the seat and their closes were within 1.6 times of each other. Medium: at least two sessions within 2.2 times. Low: anything else, often a single seat that moved a lot.
Your chances
Likely in R1 if your rank is at or under the Round 1 estimate, likely by final if under the final estimate, borderline if under the highest past close, otherwise unlikely.
Left out
PwBD, institutional (IP/INST), sponsored, foreign national and special-quota seats. The January 2027 seat matrix isn't published yet, so a college adding or cutting seats will move its close. Candidate counts for past sessions aren't known, so the estimate isn't scaled for pool size.
Sources: AIIMS New Delhi result notifications 168/2024, 01/2025, 28/2025 (Jan 2025), 327/2025, 02/2026, 69/2026 (Jan 2026), 205/2026, 216/2026, 261/2026 (Jul 2026) and 304/2026 (January 2027 qualified list). Past closing ranks guide you; they don't guarantee a seat.
